Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated May 24, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how io.them collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

2. Data We Collect

  • Audio you record in-app to talk with the assistant.
  • Text derived from speech transcription.
  • Conversation history and memory summaries stored to provide continuity.
  • Screenplay drafts, prompts, notes, and project content you create in the writing workspace.
  • Email recipient addresses you enter or select in Quick Email.
  • Basic app diagnostic data needed for reliability and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Data

  • Provide assistant responses.
  • Maintain conversation context, history, and memory features.
  • Improve safety, quality, and reliability.
  • Detect abuse and protect service integrity.

4. AI Processing and Third Parties

When you use voice/chat/writing features, your audio, text, screenplay content, and related prompts may be processed by third-party AI providers to generate transcription, responses, screenplay output, realtime voice, visual-context help, and speech output. Current provider paths may include OpenAI and ElevenLabs. These providers process data on our behalf to deliver app functionality.

Quick Email can read Contacts only after you choose to load contacts. Contact data is used to suggest recipient email addresses on-device; the selected or typed recipient address is used only to open or prepare the requested email draft.

5. Data Retention

  • Conversation and memory data are retained while your account/session remains active or until deleted.
  • Operational logs are retained for a limited period for reliability and security.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We only share data with service providers required to operate app features.

7. Your Choices

  • You can stop providing audio by not using voice input.
  • You can request deletion of stored conversation and memory data.
  • You can avoid Contacts access by not tapping Load Contacts in Quick Email, or revoke Contacts access in system settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data in transit and at rest.

9. Children

io.them is not directed to children under 13.

10. Contact

For privacy requests, contact privacy@them.io.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
